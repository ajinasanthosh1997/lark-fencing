Modernise your boundary

Keep your posts. Replace the old panels.

SmartFence can retrofit between existing concrete H-posts and over suitable gravel boards, making it a practical way to replace worn timber panels.

The PVC plastisol coating provides corrosion resistance and UV protection. Panels can also be cut to fit bays narrower than the standard 1.8m width.
