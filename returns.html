01

Change-of-mind returns

If you buy in person, there is no automatic right to a refund simply because you change your mind. For most online or telephone purchases, consumers generally have a 14-day cooling-off period from receipt of the goods. Exceptions can apply, including personalised or made-to-order products.

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights where goods are faulty, damaged, incorrectly supplied, or not as described.

02

Items that cannot normally be returned

Unless faulty, damaged, incorrectly supplied, or otherwise covered by a statutory right, we cannot normally accept returns of:

  • Cut-to-size timber or materials.
  • Special-order or made-to-order products.
  • Clearance or end-of-line items.
  • Products that have been installed, altered, or used.

03

Damaged or incorrect goods

Please inspect your order upon delivery. If it arrives damaged or incorrect, notify LARK Fencing Ltd as soon as possible and preferably within 48 hours of delivery so that we can investigate promptly. Photographs of the damage, goods, and product packaging may be required to process the claim.

Where goods are confirmed as damaged, faulty, or supplied incorrectly, we will arrange an appropriate replacement, repair, or refund at no additional cost. The 48-hour request does not remove your statutory rights.

04

Faulty products

If a product develops a manufacturing fault, contact us as soon as possible. Where appropriate, the item may be inspected before the available remedy is confirmed.

Irish consumer law provides remedies for goods that do not conform to the sales contract. Depending on the circumstances, these may include repair, replacement, a price reduction, or termination of the contract. This policy does not affect those statutory rights.

05

Installed products

Products that have been installed, altered, misused, accidentally damaged, or affected by normal wear and tear cannot be returned under our change-of-mind policy. We recommend checking all materials before installation. This does not exclude a remedy where a product itself is faulty or was incorrectly supplied.

06

Refund processing

Once an accepted return has been received and inspected, refunds will normally be processed within 5–10 working days. Any shorter statutory refund deadline will continue to apply where relevant.

Delivery charges are normally non-refundable unless the return is due to our error, the goods are faulty, or consumer law requires otherwise.

07

How to return an item

Contact our team before returning an item and provide:

  • Your name and order number.
  • Your contact details.
  • A description of the item or items.
  • The reason for the return.
  • Photographs, where applicable.

Our team will advise you of the next steps and provide return instructions.
